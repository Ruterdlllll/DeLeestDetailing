What we do

Our services

This is our base offering — more specialist services will follow. Every treatment is carried out by hand, with premium products and an obsession for the end result.

For your car

01

Interior cleaning

A complete interior treatment: vacuuming, upholstery and carpets, dashboard, trim and glass. Step into a fresh, clean cabin.

02

Exterior cleaning

A thorough hand wash with deep-cleaned rims, tyres and wheel arches, door shuts and windows. The outside of your car, spotless.

03

Polishing

Machine polishing that removes swirls, haze and light scratches, bringing back a deep, mirror-like gloss your paintwork hasn't shown in years.

04

Ceramic coating

Long-lasting paint protection with extreme gloss and a strong hydrophobic effect. Dirt slides off, washing gets easier, and the finish stays sharper for longer.

For your motorcycle

01

Full deep clean

A complete deep clean of your motorcycle — engine, frame, wheels, chain and every hard-to-reach spot. Careful, paint-safe and finished to perfection.

Your chain, your choice

After cleaning and degreasing the chain, you decide which lubricant we apply — matched to how and where you ride.

Wet lubricant

Stays put in rain and on long rides. Maximum protection, ideal for all-season and touring riders.

Dry lubricant

Clean finish that attracts less dust and dirt. Perfect for fair-weather riding and a spotless look.
